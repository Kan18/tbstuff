Half Measures 1v1

Roll a shareable pool of 10 random towers for a round.

This page is sponsored by Tower Battles Practice — join the server for practice tournaments!

Tournament Rules

Towers & Restrictions

  • Farm is forced and always takes slot one.
  • 10 random towers are rolled from the obtainable pool (Plasma Trooper and unobtainable Exclusives never appear).
  • Players choose their towers in this order:
    • Player 1 picks 1 tower
    • Player 2 picks 2 towers
    • Player 1 picks 2 towers
    • Player 2 picks 2 towers
    • Player 1 picks 1 tower
  • Once a tower is chosen, it cannot be chosen again by the other player.
  • If players cannot agree on who will pick first, a host decides by coinflip.

Maps

  • The allowed maps are Savannah, Farm Fields, Castle, and City.
  • Way Out is banned.
