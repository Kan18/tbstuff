Tournament Rules

Towers & Draft

  • Farm is forced and always takes slot one.
  • 10 random towers are rolled from the obtainable pool (Plasma Trooper and unobtainable Exclusives never appear).
  • Players alternate picking one tower at a time from the pool. Once a tower is chosen, it cannot be chosen again by the other player.
  • Draft continues until both players have 4 different towers + Farm (8 picks total, 2 unpicked).
  • If players cannot agree on who picks first, a host decides by coinflip.

Maps

  • Only maps with 137–146 stud length are allowed (Savannah is the smallest, City is the largest).
  • Way Out is banned.
  • If no allowed map appears during map selection, everyone must rejoin. If a banned map gets picked, everyone must leave immediately.
  • Official map lengths are as stated on the map selection screen.
