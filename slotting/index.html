Slotting the Slots 1v1 — restriction roller

Roll three random loadouts, then build a legal loadout from the towers that appeared. Farm is always in slot one.

Rules

  • Loadouts have 5 slots. Farm is always used and takes the first slot.
  • You may only use towers that appear in one of the three rolled loadouts.
  • Anarchist, Plasma Trooper, Hallowboomer and unobtainable towers never appear.
  • −1 slot each: Golden Scout, Mortar, Cryo-Gunner, Shotgunner, Flamethrower, Marksman.
  • −2 slots each: Commander, DJ, Enforcer.
  • If a slot-costing tower appears in more than one of the three rolls, its cost is reduced by 1 (a −2 tower can never drop below −1).
